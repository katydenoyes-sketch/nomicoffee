The face behind the cart

Katy —
PASSIONATE
about coffee.

Certified barista · Luxury events · Japan · Southwest France

"It took me years to find the perfect combination — luxury events, specialty coffee, and the Japanese art of matcha. Nomi Coffee is that combination."

After years in luxury events, I took a one-way ticket to Japan. That's where I discovered Tokyo's kissaten — traditional Japanese cafés where every cup is a ritual — and the art of matcha in its purest forms.

Back in France, I retrained as a professional barista. Today I combine these two worlds in Nomi Coffee: the precision of specialty coffee, the gentleness of Japanese matcha, and the standards of high-end event service.

Southwest France as home base. All of France as my playground.

Do you travel anywhere in France?+
I'm based in Southwest France (Biarritz, Basque Country), but I travel anywhere in France. Travel costs beyond 30 km are included in the quote — no surprises on the invoice.
Does the cart need power and water on site?+
No. 100% self-sufficient setup: onboard water tank (19L), waste tank (26L), portable battery. No infrastructure required on your end. If a 220V outlet is available — great. If not, we manage.
How far in advance should I book?+
For weddings: 6 to 12 months ahead, dates fill up fast between May and September. For other events: 4 to 6 weeks is enough. Send me a message to check my availability.
How does payment work?+
A 30% deposit at signing secures the date. Balance due 15 days before. Bank transfer or check. Registered sole trader — VAT not applicable (art. 293 B of the French Tax Code).
How many drinks per hour?+
Solo, 25 to 35 drinks per hour comfortably. For large events (200+ guests), I can bring an additional barista — just mention it in your request.
Any caffeine-free options?+
Of course — matcha latte, hot chocolate, decaf on request. Matcha has actually become the star of most of my events!
Can we customize the cups?+
Yes — your name, logo, or message printed on the cups. Production time 2 to 3 weeks. Pricing on request depending on quantity.
Have a special request?+
We love a challenge. Signature menu, an exclusive drink for your brand, an unusual format — send us a message and we'll find a solution. Our only limit is your imagination.

Nomi Coffee is an event coffee cart based in Biarritz, in the Basque Country. Katy, a certified barista, serves specialty coffee, ceremonial grade matcha and latte art for weddings, corporate seminars, pop-ups and brand activations.

The cart travels anywhere in France — from Biarritz to Paris, from Bordeaux to Lyon — with a La Marzocco Linea Mini machine and a Eureka Mignon Specialita grinder. On the menu: espressos, flat whites, cappuccinos, lattes and matcha lattes, with oat milk on request. 100% self-sufficient setup, installed in 1h30.
